Survival

Rest at Ember Shrines to refill your flask, restore health and mana, and resurrect the slain. Shrines are checkpoints: dying returns you to the last shrine you lit, and enemies stay dead until you rest again.

Stagger is your true resource. Heavy blows, parries, and spells break an enemy's poise; broken foes take critical damage from every strike until they recover. Combos build momentum — the third hit of a chain hits far harder.

Enemies scale with your level, so spend stat points and skill points whenever you have them. You keep your level, gear and quests when you die: only ground is lost.

Five Lords hold shards of the Sun Forge. Beyond them, at the Forge itself, three endings wait. What you do with the shards — and to those who wronged you — decides which.
